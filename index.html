I have hobbies

I believe you get to be no more than five things at any time. Husband, father, and work are locked into the top three spots. The last two spots…those rotate through an ever-growing list of projects (though I do tend to complete things).

I learn something new from each of them. This site shares a few of those, and maybe some lessons learned along the way.

Projects

  • 01
    KnuthFamily.com Web
    Knuth Family website following the descendants of Carl and Eva Knuth of West Prussia, circa 1870. AI coded, database-independent static site generator. Data maintained by RootsMagic. Integrated DigiKam photo database and facial recognition. Cloudflare-hosted (Pages, Workers, and D1).
  • 02
    Thingiverse Designs 3D Printing
    A collection of 3D-printable parts and designs I've posted to Thingiverse over the years.
  • 03
    RadioShift Raspberry Pi
    An FM radio with DVR-style time-shifting. Pause, rewind, and fast-forward analog broadcasts. Perfect for syncing sporting events on over-the-air TV or streaming providers with FM radio broadcasts, with a nearly unlimited buffer.
  • 04
    Pick21 Solitaire iOS
    Free solitaire game for iPhone and iPad, modeled on the old MegaTouch bar-top games. No ads, no tracking. Nearly 100% vibe coded as an experiment, but definitely NOT AI slop!
  • 05
    T-Rux Audio
    Make your own custom Teddy Ruxpin tapes. Read a book. Make it sing Mr Roboto. Creep out your neighbors or small children! Generates the original PPM motor-control track from any audio file. Grubby support coming soon!
  • 06
    Custom Pinball Pinball
    Pinball is great, and custom pinball is even better. There's a vibrant community around it that's full of creators with different skillsets. My contribution is mainly in the form of resurrecting Brooks & Dunn — Gottlieb's last machine — from an unreleased prototype playfield and a bucket of random parts. Hundreds of hours of wiring, figuring out undocumented mechs, fabricating missing parts, and writing most of the rules and code brought this machine back to life, built on Fast Pinball hardware and Mission Pinball Framework.
  • 07
    Ultra Shuffle Bowl Arcade
    A rebuilt shuffleboard bowling machine, with new retro-style artwork inspired by the ball returns in The Big Lebowski. These are electromechanical works of art in every way.
  • 08
    Electronic Dartboard Legacy
    Built way back in Visual Basic .NET to see if I could replicate the electronic dart boards found in bars. It works, though you probably can't use it. Some day I'll update to use a real dartboard and computer vision.
